How to play

  • Use the big buttons on iPad, or arrow keys on desktop, to steer sister snake around the grid.
  • Eat the 🐱 cats to grow longer and increase your score.
  • Avoid walls and your own snake body. Big sister face confidence does not prevent bonking.
  • Turn silly mode on if you want the cat snack to occasionally wiggle to a nearby square.
