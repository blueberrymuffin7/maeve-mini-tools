The Dinner Where Everyone Lied

Vale House. A storm. A study door. One half-truth too polished.

At 22:05, Dr. Elias Vale is found dead in his private study, beyond a glass greenhouse slick with rain. The power failed between 21:10 and 21:40. Six guests give six imperfect stories. Your task is not to read faces. Your task is to make the evidence speak.

Learning note: This is a fictional learning game, not a forensic lie-detection tool. The best deductions come from evidence, contradictions, motive, and timeline.
A suspect can lie without being the killer. A clue can reveal a lie without revealing the central lie. The strongest accusation needs route + motive + contradiction.
Setting

Vale House, an old estate where a glass greenhouse connects to Dr. Elias Vale’s private study.

Victim

Dr. Elias Vale, 58. Psychology professor and author of the rumored manuscript The Honest Lie.

Timeline

Blackout begins at 21:10. Estimated death is 21:25–21:40. Body discovered at 22:05.

Main question

Who lied about the crucial time period, what exactly was the lie, and why did they lie?

Psychology warning

Do not rely on nervousness, eye contact, stammering, emotional behavior, or “microexpressions” as proof. Stress and guilt can look similar from the outside. Memory is not a recording. Evidence conflicts matter more than vibes.

Investigation method

Let each person commit to a story before confronting them with key evidence. Look for selective omission, self-serving bias, and claims that cannot coexist with objective records.
